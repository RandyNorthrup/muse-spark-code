# Code of conduct

Everyone taking part in this project, in issues, pull requests, reviews and
discussions, is expected to be respectful and constructive. Disagree with
ideas, not people; assume good faith; keep criticism specific and about
the work.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks and publishing others'
private information are not tolerated. The maintainer may edit, remove or
lock contributions that break this, and may block repeat offenders.

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through the repository's
private vulnerability reporting form
(https://github.com/RandyNorthrup/muse-spark-code/security/advisories/new,
mark it "conduct") or by email to the address on the maintainer's GitHub
profile. Reports are handled in confidence.
