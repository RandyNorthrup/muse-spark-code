## 2. Summary

- **Models.** 134 in the live router list, all in §4 (one row each). 67
  have no snapshot row; 14 have a snapshot context larger than at least one
  of their providers' windows; 9 snapshot ids are no longer listed.
- **Counts** (class tags on the 141 classified rows of §3-§4; a row may
  carry two): **(a) 54, (b) 16, (c) 70, (d) 1**. Itemised changes (§5):
  2 (b), 2 (c), 1 (d).
- **Most important mismatches.**
  1. **Windows and prices are per provider**: the router picks a provider,
     and the list gives each provider's `context_length` and price. The
     product keeps one snapshot value per model; where it exceeds the chosen
     provider's window, a request can be refused (14 models).
  2. **Billing**: PRO users and Team/Enterprise seats get $2.00 of monthly
     compute credits; Free users need purchased credits; Hugging Face passes
     provider prices through "with no additional fees"; organizations bill
     with `X-HF-Bill-To` (PRICING). The product sends no `X-HF-Bill-To` and
     reads no credit balance.
  3. **No rate-limit documentation** on the pages read; the product applies
     Meta's per-minute reader (`pacing.ts:30-44`).
