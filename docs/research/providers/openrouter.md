## 2. Summary

- **Models.** The live list has **470** models; all 470 are in §5 (one row
  each, same columns as a per-model table: value, source, product, class —
  470 separate tables would be ~3,800 lines). 303 have a snapshot row; 167 do
  not. 20 snapshot ids are no longer in the live list (§5.2).
- **Counts** (class tags on the 504 classified rows of §3-§5; a row may
  carry two): **(a) 255, (b) 78, (c) 169, (d) 2**. Itemised changes (§4):
  3 (b), 3 (c), 2 (d).
- **Most important mismatches.**
  1. **167 live models have no request-time record** (no snapshot row):
     context unknown, output 32,768 (`ModelApiHost.ts:4452-4455`), although
     the live list the scan already reads states both.
  2. **Snapshot drift since 2026-10-05**: on the 303 matched models, 5
     contexts, 30 maximum outputs and 50 prices differ from the live list
     (§5); the product uses the stale values at request time.
  3. **Time-of-day prices**: 80 models carry `pricing.overrides` by UTC day
     and hour (e.g. `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017` doubles 01:00-04:00
     UTC on weekdays); the product reads one flat price.
  4. **Expiring models**: 18 models carry an `expiration_date`; the product
     shows none.
  5. **Errors and retry**: OpenRouter: retry 429, 503 and 402 with
     `limit_source: openrouter_in_flight_budget` after `Retry-After`; other
     402s, 400, 401, 403 are not wait-and-retry (ERR). The product retries
     with Meta's table (429, 500, 502, 503, 504; `transport.ts:1087`) and its
     Chat-format `parseError` keeps no code (`configured.ts:638-640`), so a
     402 insufficient-credits answer is not retried (402 is never retried,
     `retryPolicy.ts:149-151`) — right — but a 402 in-flight-budget answer is
     not retried either, and 502 (model down) is retried.
  6. **Rate limits**: `:free` models 20 RPM and 50 / 1,000 requests per day
     (by credits purchased); successful responses carry no `X-RateLimit-*`
     headers, platform 429s carry `X-RateLimit-Limit`, `-Remaining`, `-Reset`
     (LIMITS); the product reads `x-ratelimit-*` with Meta's per-minute reader
     on every response (`pacing.ts:30-44`).
