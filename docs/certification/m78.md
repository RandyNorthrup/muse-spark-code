IDE output, check and `then_run` output and a child's results cannot list

  withheld.
- **P2-1.** `subagent_wait`, `read_result` and `status` carry their
  children's spawn revisions (`childResultsTouched`).
- **P2-2, P3-1, P3-2, P3-3.** IDE, shell (and background finals), MCP,
  `run_checks` and `then_run` outcomes are incomplete: any file-policy or
  trust change while they ran refuses them.
- **P2-3.** `addIndexLine` (`src/core/memory/memoryStore.ts`) logs, then
  throws on what its owner's assertion threw; `add` wraps it in

- **P2-4.** The `none` row is machine-checked: the fixture counts every fake

## RV78f review fix: the fence at its two choke points — 2026-10-02

The follow-up review (RV78f) found one P1, three P2 and one P3. P1: an
`edit_image` source denied during the paid reservation's awaits was still
read and sent to Meta. P2: `read_skill` consulted no file rule; MCP and IDE
results had no fence after their await; no regression covered the image
and PDF reads the fence claimed. P3: a Stop during a memory read was
reported as a file error. The lead's decision: fencing tool by tool keeps
missing the next tool, so the fence moves to the two places every path
crosses (all in `src/core/backends/modelapi/ModelApiHost.ts` unless named).

- **The dispatcher's fence.** `runCall` passes every outcome through
  `fencedOutcome`, synchronously, after the call (and `touchPath`) and right
  before `finishCall` builds the outcome for the model; a background shell's
  end passes it too. It asks `policyRefusal` over the call's `Admission`
  (query, judgement after any card, and now `isTrusted`) and the files the
  outcome names in `ToolOutcome.touched` (`tools.ts`). Every path that runs a
  call fills an `AdmissionSlot` at admission: the general path in
  `decideAndRun`, `decideAndRunMemory`, `decideAndRunWebFetch` and
  `decideAndRunRename`. Tools report what they read or wrote: `read_file`
  (with its extra root, and the path it recorded as seen), `list_files`,
  `search`, `write_file`, `edit_file`, `read_skill`, the image tools, memory
  notes, `run_checks`' files, the files code intelligence's live guard let it
  read, and the files a rename passed. A tool that names none (a shell, an
  MCP or IDE tool, a web fetch, a question) is judged on verdict, mode and
  trust alone. A refusal replaces the outcome, so nothing reaches the model;
  a read's `seen` entry is forgotten; a call that had already written gets
  `policyChangedAfterWrite`, which keeps its patch on the row and tells the
  model the change stays (`UI_TEXT.policyChangedKeptWrite`, English and 14
  tables). A rejection (`isRejected`) brought nothing back and keeps its own
  words. `readFence` is removed from `ToolContext`: the per-tool post-read
  judgement was the same check at the same moment.
- **The egress fence.** `makeImage` judges the call again with its target and
  every source inside `admitAttempt`, last, after the claim check: the client
  then builds the body and calls fetch with no await. A refusal throws before
  anything is sent; `makeImage` returns it, the file reservation is released
  and the paid claim settles at zero.
- **Side effects keep their own fence** at the moment they happen: the shell's
  process entry (`runShellCall`) and each memory write
  (`runFencedMemoryCall`). A memory read is the dispatcher's, over the note's
  names.
- **`read_skill`** confines `.agents/skills/<id>/SKILL.md` and refuses it with
  `pathDeniedByPolicy` when the rules deny it, before the body is returned.
- **P3.** `MemoryStore.readNote` (`src/core/memory/memoryStore.ts`) asserts its
  owner outside the I/O error handler, and `add`/`edit` rethrow what the
  owner's assertion threw (`ownerAssertion`): a Stop takes the host's stop
  path; a policy refusal still becomes the refusal.
