## What was built

New `src/core/judge/embedding/**`, a pure core library with injected
dependencies (no `vscode`, no store, no ledger, no bundle wiring — assembly
and admission adapters stay with lane Z):

- `schema.ts` — injected `EmbeddingLimits`; head identity as P0's profile
  identity pinned to `embedding` plus the shared vector dimension; labelled
  dataset with fit/tuning/evaluation grouped splits; SHA-256 head digest;
  global-storage-only weight envelope (a bundle/VSIX location never parses).
- `fit.ts` — local opt-in logistic-head training on the fit split only
  (deterministic batch gradient descent); minimum-label, degenerate-data
  and convergence refusals; tuning/evaluation partitions recorded untouched.
- `route.ts` — fast-path run: the head answers only all-`noul` requests
  with confidence at or above the P0 floor; every mismatch, corrupt weight,
  low confidence, blocked leg or non-`noul` question falls back to the
  allowed full judge under its normal admission. The head alone never
  answers: a missing allowed full source is `source-unavailable` with no
  spend. Embedding and fallback legs each reserve their own worst-case cost.

Tests `test/unit/judgeEmbeddingFit.test.ts` (13) and
`test/unit/judgeEmbeddingRoute.test.ts` (15) with the shared fakes in
`test/unit/judgeEmbeddingFixtures.ts`; labelled synthetic fixtures in
`test/fixtures/judge/embedding/` (`labels.json`, `README.md`). No new UI
strings were needed: verdicts reuse the existing `embedding` technique and
`uncalibrated` label, refusals reuse existing reason codes, so all 14
`l10n` tables are untouched and `check:l10n` passes unchanged.
