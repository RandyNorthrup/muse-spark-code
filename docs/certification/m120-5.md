Branch `c/m120-5`, from `caea68253` (m120/redesign: accepted lanes 0 + S).
Built on the Mac rig on 2026-10-11. PLAN.md D103 (19076-19086) and M120.

## What the lane adds

- `src/core/team/outcomes.ts`: pure functions over the lane-0 record.
  - `teamTaskOutcomeSchema` / `recordOutcome`: folds one reviewed task
    (rounds to acceptance, one entry per finding, duration, redesign flag)
    into its role and candidate record. A record for another role or
    candidate is refused, never merged.
  - `suggestRoleReorder`: suggests a reorder only when one candidate is
    clearly better: both it and the candidate it passes have at least
    `TEAM_LEARNED_MIN_TASKS` (5, shared with D75's learned values) tasks,
    and its first-review acceptance rate leads by
    `TEAM_OUTCOME_MIN_RATE_GAP` (0.25). Candidates below the bar keep
    their slots. Deterministic on every platform (no locale compare).
  - `applyRoleAutoOrder`: applies the suggestion only while the role's
    preference list has `autoOrder: true`; anything else returns the list
    unchanged.
- `src/shared/teamPreference.ts`: `autoOrder` on the preference list,
  optional and absent-off, so a D75 role config still parses to the same
  value (lane-0 contract test unmodified, green).
- `src/shared/constants.ts`: `TEAM_OUTCOME_MIN_RATE_GAP`.
- `src/shared/l10n/en.ts` + all 14 `l10n/ui.*.json`: `teamOutcomeReorderReason`
  ("{better}: {betterAccepted} of {betterTasks} {role} tasks accepted on
  first review; …"), read at use time through `fill`.
