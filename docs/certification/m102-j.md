## Journal, filesystem and export handoff

- `src/core/usage/journalStore.ts`: `UsageFs`, `UsageLock`,
  `UsageJournalStore`, `UsageJournalRead`, `UsageRollupRow`, its checked
  row schema, and `rollupUsageRecords`. Each process injects one unique
  `writerId` (e.g. `randomUUID()`), its clock, a runtime history-setting getter
  and a fixed-code logger. `noteUsage(usage, context)` contains the whole tap;
  `append(entry)` accepts a usage or limit entry. Both return immediately and
  contain failures. `flush()` drains the queue for shutdown.
- `read()` returns `records`, `limits`, `rollups`, `recordCount`,
  `newerVersionRecords`, `newerVersionFiles`, `tornLines` and `invalidLines`. Raw files reuse parsed
  entries and read only appended bytes, including completion of a torn UTF-8
  tail. Truncation or same-size mtime changes reload a file. Unchanged monthly
  rollups reuse their checked rows; replacements reload them. Future raw
  versions are counted without returning their content. An unsupported
  rollup version increments `newerVersionFiles` and stops maintenance; its
  internal record count stays unknown and is never added to `recordCount` or
  `newerVersionRecords`. S can surface the existing `unsupported` error code
  while still showing the known records.
- `retain(historyDays = 365)` keeps 30 inclusive local dates detailed, then
  writes checked monthly rollups atomically before deleting raw days. Covered
  raw days are ignored, including after a crash between publication and
  deletion. Torn/invalid/future days inside retention stay raw. Rows preserve
  certainty dimensions, known optional sums, units, latency histograms,
  duration/first-token sample counts, retries and rate-limit counts. Limit
  snapshots survive rollup. Retention is by the stored calendar date, so DST
  and timezone moves do not rebucket it. Out-of-history days/months expire.
- `reset()` takes no path and deletes only `usage/`. Its caller owns the
  count-and-confirm modal. Tests keep budget-journal, paid-daily, tab-spend
  and paid-grants proof files byte-identical. Both raw and monthly caches clear.
- `src/runtime/usage/nodeUsageFs.ts`: the real Node adapter, rooted at the
  injected `agentDataFolder`, with namespace/traversal checks, linked-folder
  refusal, private directories/append files, positioned reads, the existing
  `writeFileAtomically` helper and exclusive-create maintenance locks. Stale
  locks, empty locks left before the token write, and abandoned recovery
  markers recover after the timeout. A former owner cannot release its
  successor. The filesystem has Node's usual check-to-mutation race boundary;
  it does not claim a conditional unlink or protection against another process
  maliciously replacing the private tree between checks.
- `src/core/usage/usageExport.ts`: `exportUsageCallsCsv(journal, range, now)`
  accepts only detailed ranges; `exportUsageSummaryCsv(pageState)` encodes the
  exact checked page aggregates, with group counters once and certainty costs
  on separate rows; `exportUsageJson(journal, range)` includes versioned raw
  records, snapshots, old rollup rows and reader diagnostics. `usageCsvCell`
  quotes cells and prefixes spreadsheet formula starters. These functions
  return text; save/download/CLI `--out` remain the host/service's responsibility.

No production fake, new dependency, suppression or unverifiable cast was
introduced. The only production price-card change is the owned 1-hour cache
write fix. Source/command/setting registration, recording taps and editor routes
remain with their named lanes. Integrators should inject the writer through
its port from the lazy usage service; importing the full store into a startup
bundle would defeat the planned writer-size allowance.
