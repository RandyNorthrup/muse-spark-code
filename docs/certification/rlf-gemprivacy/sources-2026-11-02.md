## 1. Gemini storage default + `store` opt-out

URL: <https://aistudio.google.com/docs/changelog> (read 2026-11-02)

> ### 10-26-2026
>
> **GenerateContent storage is on by default for new paid projects.** New
> projects with billing enabled now store prompts and responses for 30 days
> to power Logs and Datasets. Existing projects keep their current setting.
> Opt out per request with `store=false`, or change the default in the Logs
> and Datasets settings.

(read 2026-11-02), request-body field list:

> **store**: `boolean`
> Optional. Configures the logging behavior for a given request. If set, it
> takes precedence over the project-level logging config.

## 2. Gemini reroutes

URL: <https://aistudio.google.com/docs/changelog> (read 2026-11-02)

> ### 10-08-2026
>
> **Gemini 3.5 Flash and Gemini 3.7 Flash are now deprecated.** Requests to
> `gemini-3.5-flash` are automatically routed to `gemini-3.6-flash`, and
> requests to `gemini-3.7-flash` to `gemini-3.8-flash`. ... Neither endpoint
> shuts down, so your existing calls keep working, billed at the new
> models' rates.

> ### 11-02-2026
>
> **Gemini 2.5 Pro and Gemini 2.5 Flash move to the 3.x lineup.** Starting
> December 1, 2026, requests to `gemini-2.5-pro` route to
> `gemini-3.8-flash` and requests to `gemini-2.5-flash` route to
> `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`. ... Requests are billed at the serving model's
> rates, and the old ids keep working until we announce otherwise.

## 3. OpenAI `shutdown_date` semantics

URL: <https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/deprecations> (read 2026-11-02)

> The response includes an optional field, `shutdown_date`, which contains
> the shutdown date as a `YYYY-MM-DD` string, or `null` if no shutdown date
> has been announced. We recommend monitoring this field to stay up to date
> on model deprecations.

> When we announce that a model or endpoint is being deprecated, it
> immediately becomes deprecated. All deprecated models and endpoints will
> also have a shut down date, which is the date they will no longer be
> available.

Announced dates read the same day: 2027-01-14 for the gpt-4o dated
snapshots (among others); 2027-04-01 for gpt-5.3-codex, gpt-5.4-nano
and gpt-5.1. No shutdown date has been announced for any GPT-6 model.
