**Residual (documented, not closed).** The `SecretStore` port (and the
keychain behind it) has no compare-and-swap. A 401 handler or a refused
refresh reads the item and then deletes it; a pair another process

cross-process lock or an atomic store, which neither the keychain binding
nor the M109 vault port offers today.
