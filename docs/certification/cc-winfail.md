## 4. The open question

The two `runtimeLsp` hook cases pass on a quiet PC (2.4 s and 3.7 s at the last
run) and fail when the PC is saturated, as in the lead's full run, because a
Windows hook launch costs about 2 s against Tab's 1.5 s bound. Options:

1. Leave it. They pass here alone, and hosted CI (the gate) decides on a quiet
   machine; they were seen to fail only in a full local run while the PC was
   busy.
2. A separate change to the Windows job runner that cuts the launch's PowerShell
   starts (about ten per hook), proved through the packed product. It is the
   only change that makes Tab hooks usable on a busy Windows PC, not just the
   test.
3. Raise `TAB_HOOK_TIMEOUT_MS` or exempt the launch from it. Rejected here:
   the bound is Tab's latency design, and raising a limit only to pass a test
   is what the brief forbids.

Recommendation: 1 now, 2 as its own lane.
