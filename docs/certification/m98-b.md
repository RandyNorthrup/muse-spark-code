## Delivered scope

Only new calibration modules, `judgeCalibration` tests, calibration fixtures,
and this receipt are changed. Existing phase-1 files are byte-identical.
The base lacks completed phase-1 A/S/U/G/D integration; the brief explicitly
allows this offline lane on the phase-2 planning base. These pure functions
do not wire into a backend, activate a profile, load on activation, or change
the single-model user's behaviour.

- `schema.ts`: strict zod validation for local labelled prediction datasets
  and persisted profiles. Binary labels only. `risk.destructive` accepts
  independent hand/M75 labels; `skill.fit` accepts M75 routing labels.
  Outcome signals, reviewer responses and model beliefs are rejected, and no
  optional-signal store is added. Identifiers must be opaque, not source text.
- Fit, tuning and evaluation are disjoint by both task and repository.
  Sample IDs and factual label IDs cannot be reused. All splits are required.
- `fit.ts`: bounded, regularized binary Platt fitting with stable logit,
  sigmoid, cross entropy and Newton backtracking. One-class/constant inputs
  and failed convergence give explicit uncalibrated reason codes. Isotonic
  pools adjacent violations after aggregating equal probabilities, and
  requires at least 200 independent **fit** labels in the family.
- Profiles bind model/version, endpoint/source, technique, family,
  prompt/criteria and fit version. Initial techniques are `logprob` and
  `stated` only; top-1, sampling/contrastive and other families give explicit
  unsupported reasons. Those techniques can gain separately measured
  support later without inheriting a stated profile.
- The cascade reliability weight is inverse calibrated tuning Brier,
  bounded by the injected weight floor. It is learned from tuning only;
  evaluation labels never change parameters or weights. This choice supplies
  2a with non-equal reliability weights, but has no adoption claim before its
  paired M75 measurement.
- `report.ts`: machine-readable ECE (equal-width positive-probability bins)
  and binary Brier from evaluation only, the uncalibrated arm, and all three
  sample counts. The report validates the persisted profile's exact split
  membership. It returns an evaluated profile without changing its weights.
- `apply.ts`: no calibrated label until independently labelled evaluation
  exists for the exact profile. Synthetic evidence, partial results,
  mismatched identities and corrupt probabilities/profiles remain
  uncalibrated. The offline transform itself confers no label.

The `independent` evidence marker is a local dataset provenance declaration,
not an authenticity proof. Z/G must supply trusted independently labelled
predictions and the separately authorized counted capture receipt. These
fake tests establish mechanics only; no empirical calibration or product
activation is certified. B performs no I/O, logging, network calls or stores.
