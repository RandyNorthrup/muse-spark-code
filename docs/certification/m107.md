## RVM107W1B: open-intent redesign and the contrast qualification

2026-10-08. Codex re-review RVM107W1B of `23432ce4d..8af2be222` confirmed
P2-1 and P2-3 fixed. It found two lifecycle gaps left in P2-2 and one P3.
P2-2 has now had two review rounds, so the open-intent mechanism is
redesigned, not patched. No live or paid model call: **0 attempts**.

**Design.** The adapter keeps one record per surface document. A Reload
(`webviewSetup.ts` `reload`) reports `onDocumentReplaced`, which starts a new,
unready generation; only that document's `ready` makes it ready again. Show
binds its open to exactly one target when it runs. That target is either the
surface in view, or, when none exists, the surface id a new conversation
opens in (`startConversation` returns it before the surface can be ready:
`SIDEBAR_SURFACE_ID`, or a panel id passed into `openChatPanel`). The open is
sent at once to a ready document of its target, otherwise held. It is
delivered once, to that target's first ready generation, never to another
surface. Removing the target cancels it; disposing the adapter cancels it,
ignores later readiness callbacks and drops the registry listener. A second
Show replaces the first, so its target gets one open. This replaces
`isOpenPendingForNew` and the two WeakSets.

**Regressions, all failing on `8af2be222`** (copied into a detached worktree

  Shows, then status and one open": ready → reload → Show → Show → ready

  is ready first": another surface that becomes ready first gets the status
