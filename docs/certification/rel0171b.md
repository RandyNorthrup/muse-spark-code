## Findings fixed on the branch

### Pressed menu pills looked like hovered ones (M114 P2)

`m114Panel.test.mjs` failed all 12 theme/forced-colour cases of "enabled
approval decisions and crisp pills distinguish hover from pressed" after
the menu merge (linuxlt): menu-fan had given hover, pressed and selected
pills one style and dropped the pressed pill's inset ring. The ring could
not simply return: each pill's inline `outline-offset` (the tight-fan focus
pull) overrode the stylesheet, which turned the restored ring into an
outer one that overlapped its neighbour (the restructured outline check
below failed every `focus+active` pair: gap −2 px).

Fix `a64d9b641`: the pill sets `--ms-gooey-focus-offset`, which only the two
focus-visible rules read, and the pressed rule's 2 px ring at −2 px offset
applies again. After: `m114Panel` 71/71, `m114Conversation` 31/31,
`GooeyMenu` 23/23, `gooeyLayout` 26/26, `rowMenus` 27/27, `Transcript`
64/64, `QuoteMenu` 3/3 (linuxlt). README captures recaptured: unchanged.

### Menu browser cases near the 5 s deadline

Measured alone on linuxlt (load 2.7–5.6, 8 cores), the original
`visualStability.test.mjs` took 250 s for 108 cases: 1.06–4.02 s each,
median 1.99 s. On the loaded Mac mini three cases missed the deadline.

Fix `96ab196a3` (no deadline, assertion, scene or threshold change):

- `capture.mjs` exports `openCapturePage` and `prepareScene`, the page setup
  and scene settling `captureMatrix` already ran; `captureMatrix` calls them.
- The suite opens its server, browser connection and context once, warmed
  by a first scene; each case gets a fresh page on that context. A shared
  page was tried first: one overrun case then broke 29 later ones.
- The outline check sends its forced classes and its measurement together
  on one CDP session (run in order), and alternates focused and neighbour
  pills down the stack, so two passes per state cover every adjacent pair
  in both orders (12 round trips instead of 48 for five pills). Every pill
  style depends on its own state only; each pair sees the states it saw.

Per-phase probe (linuxlt, load 7–9): page 0.21–0.46 s, measurement
0.2–0.4 s, and the harness scene itself 1.1–1.6 s (2.4–3.0 s for
`chat-menu`). Whole cases: Kubuntu load 4–7, median 1.07 s, max 2.52 s;
Kubuntu load 7–15, median 1.52 s, max 3.20 s. Residual: the scene's own
preparation (navigation, bundle, the harness's 6.5 s frozen-clock run),
the same work a visual capture does per scene, dominates. At load 30 on
linuxlt one `chat-tool-menu` scroll case still passed 5 s. The pre-existing
prefix capture hook timed out once at its 10 s hook deadline on Kubuntu
(load 5–7); five other runs passed it.

Guard evidence: the restructured outline check went red on the real
inline-offset overlap above before the fix, and green after.

rig, frozen-clock semantics unchanged). A per-phase probe of `openScene`

### Open menus captured where the opener had been

The first reviewed update (`96ab196a3`, linuxlt) showed
`chat-tool-menu-narrow`'s default frames at 320 px with the fan about 70 px
above its opener while every other state showed it centred on the opener.
The menu now follows its opener on animation frames, which the capture's
frozen clock does not run, so a row that moved after the menu opened (a lazy
part painting) left the fan behind in some captures and not others. Two
Kubuntu renders of the same source differed by 16,175 pixels
(`npm run check:visual -- --shard=287/1632`, `default/dark/320`, exit 1).
Menu-fan's own reviewed baseline had the settled frame; its round-3 shard
check covered the light group only.

Fix `cd2d3e687`: `prepareScene` re-places any open menu through its own
resize listener, lets React's render task run, and repeats until no pill
moves (at most ten tries, then a loud failure). With a temporary manifest
pointing at `cd2d3e687` (so each shard renders the same source twice), the
Kubuntu replays of shards 287 (twice), 15, 559 (twice), 1375, 344 and 208
(the narrow tool menu in four themes and the quote menu at both widths) all
exit 0. That first archive was discarded and the update was repeated.
