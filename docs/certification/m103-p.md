## Integration boundaries

Lane I's `ports.ts`, approval contract and maker constants are absent from
this base. Lane P therefore exposes explicit injected dependencies for
host approval, bounded process execution, native mutexes, limits and logs.
No production fake or standing approval is supplied. Lane W must wire these
to the frozen MakerHost contract and its lazy bundle. This is portable core
for extension, ACP and companion/plugin hosts equally.

Shared files owned by lanes 0/I/W (strings, constants, PLAN, CHANGELOG,
README, build and host-API lists) are untouched. Internal errors are stable
codes; adapters must render lane 0's localized text, including reset warnings
and busy/unknown identities. No new dependency, command or setting.

The rig brief forbids the full quality gate and reserves it for the lead.
Local commits use the existing pre-commit hooks; the lead's full quality
gate and final lazy-bundle/package checks remain required before integration.
