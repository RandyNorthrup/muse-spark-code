## Pills with labels (2026-10-04)

The owner's request: "The gooey menu should have labels with the icons so
the shape of the items will gain more of a pill shape than a circle with
icon only", and his clarification the same day: "the icon and label should
be in the same blue pill no separate". Branch `m87/pills` from `m87/l0`
`d7bb4260`. Scope: the menu's rendering, labels, layout and styles
(`GooeyMenu`'s component, `gooeyLayout.ts`, the `.gooey-menu*` CSS,
`GOOEY_MENU`). `useRowMenu` (its context-menu selection scope and the
opener's Copied state) is lane W's and was not touched. No dependency,
string, wire message or escape hatch was added.

### What changed

- **One pill per item.** Each item, and each item of a second burst, is a
  single button: the icon, then the label (`.gooey-menu-pill-label`), 40 px
  high with a 20 px radius, in the button colours (hover and focus as
  before). The separate label (`gooeyLabelBox`, `.gooey-menu-label`) and
  its hover state are gone. The accessible name stays `aria-label` = the
  label; roles, keys, focus return and the disabled-item guards are
  unchanged.
- **Notes.** A disabled item (a Rewind note) used to be drawn at 50 %
  opacity. On a pill that let the chat show through its text, so a note is
  now the same blue pill, opaque, with only its icon dimmed and no hover.
  Forced colours draw it in `GrayText`.
- **Layout: a fanned column.** On the old 76 px arc, neighbouring pills
  would overlap unless the radius grew past what a 320 px panel holds. The
  pills stack in one column 20 px apart, on the side of the origin with
  more room, centred on its height and moved whole to stay inside the
  panel. Their near ends follow half an ellipse around the origin, 16 px
  out at the ends (clear of a 26 px "…" centred there) and 48 px at the
  middle. Rows cannot overlap whatever the widths, so the layout needs each
  pill's width only to keep it inside the panel. The component measures
  every shown pill (`offsetWidth`, and whether its label overflows) in a
  layout effect before the first paint, keeping the sizes per level so an
  open group's pill keeps its place. A pill is at most the panel less 8 px
  a side (inline `max-width`). One too wide for its side of the origin
  slides back over it, and only past the cap does the label end in an
  ellipsis, with the whole label as the pill's `title`. A second burst
  centres on its group's pill and scales in from that pill's centre. The
  pills burst from the "…" button's centre (it used to be its top-right
  corner). A panel too short for 20 px gaps shares its height out evenly.
- **Goo.** The filter is unchanged. Two flat pill edges 20 px apart blur
  (σ 10) to 2Φ(−1) ≈ 0.32 alpha at the middle, under the 7/18 cut, so
  resting pills stay apart. They merge while they scale in from the origin
  and overlap. Under reduced motion there is no burst and no goo; under
  forced colours there is no filter and the pills are bordered in
  `ButtonText`.

## A fan of crisp pills, one size (2026-10-04, integration)

The owner's two follow-ups the same evening, verbatim: "the gooey menus just
need the pills in the fan/arc shape not with the faded smudge look", then
"also they should be a uniform size". Done on `m87/int`; PLAN.md D66 records
the amendment ("A fan of crisp pills, one size").

- **No goo.** `GooeyMenu.tsx` draws no SVG: the filter, its `defs`, the
  `--ms-goo-filter` property, `GOOEY_MENU.blur`, the `.gooey-menu-defs` rule
  and the reduced-motion and forced-colours rules that switched the filter
  off are gone, with the `gooey-menu-motion-safe` hook they used. A pill is
  a flat button: no blur, shadow or halo; its focus border is its only
  outline. The component keeps its name; the look is a fan of pills.
- **One size.** `gooeyPillWidth` gives every pill 272 px (320 − 2 × 8
  padding − 32 bow, the widest that keeps the whole fan inside a 320 px
  panel); the component sets that width and the 40 px height inline on each
  pill, so the drawn widths are no longer measured. The icon sits at the
  start and the label fills the rest, ending in an ellipsis when it is
  longer than the pill; it never wraps or grows the pill, and keeps its
  whole text as the accessible name and, when clipped, the tooltip.
- **The arc stays whole.** A fan that would leave the panel moves back,
  over the origin if need be, all at once (one shift for every pill), so the
  arc keeps its shape at 320 px from any origin. A panel narrower than that
  gets a shallower arc, down to a straight column; rows never overlap.
  The old per-pill clamp, which broke the arc into a ragged edge, is gone.
- **Motion.** Each pill scales in from the origin 30 ms after the one before
  it (`GOOEY_MENU.staggerMs`, inline `animation-delay`, `backwards` fill);
  under reduced motion the closing block's `animation: none` shows them in
  place at once. The harness page draws the pills at rest: headless
  Chrome's virtual clock does not run CSS animations, and the earlier
  captures caught them mid-burst, tiny and smeared by the goo (the "before"
  shots below).
