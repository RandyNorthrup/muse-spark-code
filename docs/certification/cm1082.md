  their caps. CI stdin keys never select or pool stored accounts.

  settle on the original account; nonsends refund; unknown sends retain
  liability. Pending counters survive resets; the send sets its actual day.

  concurrent resets. Corrupt, linked or oversized claim files refuse with
  fixed error text. No label, prompt, endpoint, credential or key digest goes

  binding; held revoked answers neither grant nor dispatch. Runtime metadata

  Meta's origin/product/policy mapping is fixed rather than user-overridable.

  Best-of-N passes its question through the installed runner/manager; legacy
  schedules carry their original question. Native replay crosses accounts
