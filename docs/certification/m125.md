# M125 certification

Per-lane gate-fire and wiring records for the fast judge. Nothing here
claims a live measurement: every figure below comes from a fake replay
(scripted model, scripted judge) or a unit-fed recorder, which certify
wiring only. Live runs need the owner's go with counts first.

## Lane V: measurement (2026-10-23, rig linuxlt, worktree RLM125)

### What was built

- Nine frozen judge arms (`src/core/eval/armDefinitions.ts`,
  `src/core/eval/mechanisms.ts`, cases `judge-off`, `judge-keep-off`,
  `judge-pack-off` plus one isolated arm per wired decision): the
  isolated decision runs its natural verdict, every other pins off.
  Keep's pair carries evaluated compaction and triage's M73's packing,
  since keep only fires at a compaction and triage's early mark only
  moves bytes through the packing projection.
- Four judge tasks (`JUDGE_EVAL_TASKS` in `src/core/eval/tasks.ts`):
  `judge-injected-loop`, `judge-injected-failure`, `judge-scope-slip`,
  `judge-skill-routing`, each carrying its injected conditions. The
  set grows toward `JUDGE_FAST_ACCURACY_MIN_LABELS` labelled runs.
- `JudgeFastReplayRecorder` (`src/core/eval/judgeReplay.ts`): paired
  win, wall-time, calls, tokens, precision, recall and harm per
  backend and model, with the §11 ship rule (`ships`/`blockedBy`).
- Runner wiring (`src/core/eval/runner.ts`, `src/core/eval/driver.ts`):
  the driver collects `judgeFastTurn`/`judgeFastSkip` and the turn
  wall time, passes an optional caution judge into the turn, and the
  runner records arm and decision rows and carries the judge section
  (fence plus fast) into the report and its markdown.
- `JudgeReplayRecorder` wired into the runner: fence samples reach
  the run's recorder through the advisory's `onFence`, which the live
  harness binds with its labels; the fake replay records scripted
  samples through the same path.

### Findings for the live runs

- Shell outputs always end with an exit line, so triage's
  blank-success never fires for them; the strict pass-summary does.
- Loop, done and failure queue for the next turn, so a single-turn
  eval shows their applied rates, harm and pass-safety but no wall
  win; their wins come from multi-turn production traces (lane U's
  aggregator). Triage, effort and keep win within the turn.
- Scope, skill, edit and claim carry task conditions but no arms
  until a host wires them. `risk.destructive` stays under M98's
  caution judge.
- Live runs still need: the owner's go with counts, the independent
  labelling pass (D77), and the live harness binding a real `JudgeUse`
  whose `onFence` records with its labels.

## Lane U-b: usage/panel wiring (2026-10-23, rig linuxlt, worktree RLM125)

### What was built

- `FastPanelFacts` (`src/core/judge/fast/panelFacts.ts`): the window's
  rolls over `judgeFastTurn` events — newest record per decision, window
  totals (turns, applied turns, raised, acted, judge calls and tokens),
  newest method. No turns means no facts; unwired decisions stay absent.
- `projectFastJudgePanel` (`src/shared/judgePanel.ts`): rows name acted
  above raised above absent; the saving multiplies lane V's paired
  median by applied turns, unknown on either side absent; the snapshot
  sums the measured rows. `projectJudgePanel` carries `fast` through.
- The window port (`src/host/judge/judgeBundle.ts`) holds the rolls
  outside the lazy bundle, so a fast-only window that never loads the
  caution judge still counts: it merges fast rows into the caution
  snapshot, or projects fast-only facts (billing none) when the caution
  judge never ran. The controller routes `judgeFastTurn` to the rolls
  and re-posts `judgeUsage`.
- Shared rows (`src/shared/judgePanelText.ts`) for the panel and the
  usage dialog: method, turns, applied, judge calls and tokens, saved
  time when measured, one line per evaluated decision. `formatDurationMs`
  moved to `src/shared/l10n/text.ts`; the agent views re-export it.
- `judgePanel.fast` strings in English plus all 14 tables.

### Wiring receipts
