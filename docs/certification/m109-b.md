## Policy and approval piece

- Registered requester identity, ceiling, role, workspace, session and task
  are immutable snapshots. The trusted launcher verifies registrations;
  frame claims alone confer nothing.
- Ask every time, exact-use session answers, Always with a matching scoped
  grant, Never, disclosure opt-in, provenance taint and unattended flags
  are enforced. Every grant field narrows access. Hooks never prompt.
- Requests and one-use tickets are minted by the broker, with canonical
  digest, nonce, deadline and epoch. Only an authenticated host UI answers.
  Actual use is checked again at redemption; callers and returned cards
  cannot mutate the broker's snapshot.
- Per-use OS presence is uncached. A lock while presence is pending closes
  the lifetime, erases the held/source keys and denies a late result.
- Revocation closes active lifetimes and terminates processes through the
  route's pinned process port; an unsuccessful termination is explicitly
  audited as unrecallable. First-party fallback refuses all agent uses.
- Grant use counts are reserved when minting a ticket, through C's atomic
  transaction. An unused/failed ticket does not refund that reservation.
