## Paired retention measurements — PRRT_kwDOUkzj5M6p1xVm

Monthly rows persist optional, strictly validated `measurements`: cache input
and cached-token sums, speed output and duration sums, latency/first-token sums,
and each measurement's observation count. Only calls reporting both operands
contribute to a paired measurement; known zeroes count as observations. The
production shared runtime adapter restores these measurements for every editor.
Older v1 rows without measurements still load, retain their histogram-derived
latency, and keep paired rates unknown.

The real-filesystem regression performs retention and reads through a fresh
production service, comparing totals, buckets, groups and features. Its mixed
calls include paired counters, unrelated token-only/duration-only counters,
zeroes and missing fields; persisted negative coverage is refused. The legacy
case removes measurements from the saved monthly file and reopens the service.

## Fixed-point retention costs — PRRT_kwDOUkzj5M6p1xVs

Raw aggregation's existing `usdUnits` conversion is reused by the rollup writer.
Both actual USD and subscription API-equivalent USD remain safe integer
micro-dollars during accumulation and convert to USD only when rows finish.
Missing amounts stay absent, known zeroes stay zero, and unsafe amounts or sums
are refused. The fractional regression checks two $0.0000004 calls explicitly.

Fifty seeded property trials retain real journal files and reopen the production
service. They vary dates, clients, backends, providers, models, request kinds,
all cost certainties, missing/zero token and duration counters and fractional
amounts; every current/previous total, daily bucket, breakdown and feature total
must equal raw aggregation under all four grouping modes. Only unordered cost
categories/features are sorted by identity for comparison. Each trial is its
own test, preserving the repository's five-second per-test deadline, with a
fixed service clock so fixtures cannot expire as the real date advances.
