the unchanged 50 MB/s floor. A head-to-head probe on this tree
(`test/unit/vault/zz-oldbench.test.ts`, since removed) timed HEAD's
`scrub.ts` against the T1-fixed one on identical input in one

with the file's ten preceding tests in-process; a dead-heap probe

GC/load state on this shared rig (load averages 4-7 through the
runs), not scan performance. No threshold, timeout, source, or
benchmark was changed, and no green rerun was selected: the
full-suite record below quotes what the rig measured.
