## Implemented boundary

- `selectTeamEntry` remains the public pool selector (re-exported from
  `teamPool.ts`). Its implementation lives in `selection.ts` so the picker
  and reviewer can call it without a runtime import cycle. A snapshot can
  carry lane 0's `TeamPreferenceList`, `TeamAvailabilityProbe`, task
  conditions, captured engine identity and handoff authorship. Every earlier
  refusal is a `TeamSkip`; absent preferences preserve the D75 result shape.
- `TeamLiveAvailability` reads sign-in, last-probe health, node availability,
  governor admission and cooldown facts from the installed caller. Caps,
  budgets, concurrency and rate/usage marks retain the real pool checks.
  Unknown capabilities fail requirements; zero preference caps are enforced.
- `TaskPicker` chooses only the first available configured candidate before
  applying task/conversation scoring. Its selection callback exposes skips.
  It refuses a stale worker profile/model/kind; reviewer selection also
  refuses a stale reported model. The scheduler checks again
  after authorization and releases admission without an attempt if the
  candidate's identity or availability changed.
- `pickReviewer` uses the same selector with preferences. Every writer in
  the handoff/borrow chain and its engine is excluded. Missing captured
  engine identities refuse. D75's legacy reviewer fallback remains unchanged
  when preferences are absent; the preference path never uses that fallback.
- `SchedulerBorrowing` builds D212 snapshots from ready demand, active
  attempts, assignments, future review/fix work, role time boxes, recorded
  medians and cooldowns. The real periodic sweep and event drain call both
  lane-0 decisions. Candidate availability/caps are evaluated per waiting
  task; one proposed borrow takes one agent.
- A borrow is recorded only after `TaskBoard.begin`, before journalling and
  launch. `handoff`, `unmetSinceMs`, `recallAskedAtMs` and `borrowCooldowns`
  are optional validated board data; they do not appear in legacy boards.
  The handoff schemas moved to `shared/teamHandoff.ts`, with compatible
  re-exports from `teamPreference.ts`, to avoid a shared-schema cycle.
- Lane H receives `SchedulerRecallEvent` through the required
  `SchedulerBorrowPort.recall` consumer: task, attempt, clock and the exact
  `keep`/`finish`/`recall`/`stop` decision. Demand and the anchored recall time
  are journalled before delivery. Repeated recalls retain their deadline;
  H owns safe points, stopping, capture and requeue. The event is an internal
  port, not a new unobserved MSP/HTTP shape.
- The existing lazy scheduler factory exposes the selector, live probe and
  reviewer to lane W. No surface or command was added. README, the existing
  team feature's reference link and the changelog state the remaining
  installed bindings honestly. Logic is shared by every editor/runtime.

### Design as built

- **One reservation per launch** (`LaunchReservation`, `teamPool.ts`).
  Selection reserves the slot and lease. After consent, one synchronous step
  ranks the task against live facts and counts the attempt through
  `board.begin` (turn and caps). The step also records the loan the board
  holds for that attempt. The reservation ends exactly once: consumed by
  `start`, released, or kept as the existing uncertain liability.
- **The final gate validates and never selects** (`isReservationValid`,
  `SchedulerBorrowing.validate`). After the launch journal, and with no await
  before `start`, it checks five things. The board still carries the held
  loan, unrecalled, on this attempt and running on the lender. The
  assignment, candidate and home entry still authorize it, by fingerprint and
  worker identity. The lender is live (probe and limit marks) and idle apart
  from this loan. D212's conditions still hold through `decideBorrow` on the
  task as selection saw it: the demand wait, no free main candidate, no
  main-role work queued or due within the lookahead, and the cooldown. The
  lender's turn, caps, concurrency and slot are never read again. Main
  candidates are asked whether they could take this admission in the lender's
  place. Its running place, slot and turn are set aside for that task alone,
  so a loan whose own count fills the turn cannot hide a recovered native.
  The gate writes nothing: no demand, loan, recall or roster callback.
- **Exact refund.** A refused reservation calls `board.cancelUnstarted`,
  which calls the new `BoardContext.refundAttempt` once, before it mutates
  anything. The admission returns to the queue with its queue age, earlier
  chain links and demand window kept, and its slot and lease are released. If
  the refund throws, the counted attempt stays on the board and the existing
  uncertain path keeps the liability. Pause or Stop during the journal keeps
  M96c's uncertain liability, unchanged.
- **Single-owner drain.** `wake` publishes ownership (`draining`, the joiners'
  list) before the first pass runs, so a wake re-entering from the pass's
  synchronous start joins it. `drain` loops while the latch is set and
  releases ownership in the same synchronous step that finds the latch clear,
  or immediately on a failed pass, rejecting joiners with that error. A wake
  therefore either lands before the final check and is drained, or finds no
  owner and becomes the next one.
- D212 rules and timers are unchanged: the 60 s demand floor, 10 min
  lookahead, 5 min finish grace, 2 min recall wait, 3 min cooldown, one task
  per borrow, borrowed coders excluded from their own review, queued main
  work blocking a borrow, and maximum bipartite matching for recall.

## Round 5 redesign, fix 1 (host)

Review RVM120S5C of `e87abeee8` found three P2s of one class: the final gate
read fewer facts than admission. It skipped `isAgentAvailable`
(installed/sign-in/key/provider), live occupancy outside the task board, and
the live Default identity of a lender without its own agent. Lead ruling:
extract one admission predicate and call it from selection and the gate, not

### Design

- `SchedulerBorrowing.admission(context, task, lender, lists, reservation,
facts)` is the predicate for one (task, lender, reservation) triple. It reads, in
  order: authorization (assignment, candidate, home entry, eligibility, and the
  loan's fingerprint when one is held); identity (the home entry against the
  lender's live resolved agent, via `candidateAgent`, which covers Default;
  a held attempt against the home entry); author, conditions and availability
  (`liveCandidateSkip`, now shared with `selectTeamEntry`); limit marks;
  `isAgentAvailable`; idleness (live `runningByAgent`/`runningByEntry` with
  only the held attempt set aside); and D212's lending conditions through
  `decideBorrow` (demand, recovered main candidate, main work, cooldown, board
  idleness).
- Selection calls it from the borrowing tick for every lender, before capacity
  and ranking. It also calls it from the per-pass re-validation of proposed
  loans, which the post-consent ranking uses before `board.begin` counts. The
  final gate (`validate`) checks the held reservation (`reservation`), then
  evaluates only the predicate. It sets aside the admission's own turn,
  running place and slot only for the recovered-main-candidate question. It
  answers the refusing fact, and the scheduler then refunds exactly once.
- `LOAN_FACTS` names every fact the predicate reads. The selection snapshot's
  running maps now state that they include admissions journalled but not yet
  started.
