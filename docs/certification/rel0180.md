### Two changes nobody had listed

Both were reported to the lead before this commit and accepted.

- **`accounts-swap`** (944 to 1,552 px a frame): the wording above, intended.
- **`usage-stacked`** (16,548 to 51,418 px a frame): one bucket of $0.10 was
  drawn at about a tenth of the plot's height and now fills it.
  `StackedColumns` scales with `Math.max(1, …)`, a guard against dividing by
  zero. While cost was plotted in dollars that guard also meant "at least
  $1"; since `271cccaa8` cost is plotted in whole nano-dollars, so the cost
  chart scales to its largest period as the token and time charts always
  did. The labels are unchanged ("meta: $0.10") and the chart has no axis.
  The lead's ruling: the new scaling is the intended one; `CHANGELOG.md` says
  so under 0.18.0, Changed.
