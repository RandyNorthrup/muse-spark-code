# CM352 — bounded realtime Muse Voice on Model API

2026-10-11, macmini; branch `c/m35-2`, base `dbdb55a0e` (0.17.1).

The existing VS Code-family sidebar and conversation tabs now admit Muse Voice
on either backend. No finite-cap blanket refusal remains on the realtime
microphone path. Model API uses its owned session journal when present and
requires it under a finite conversation cap. Both backends reserve D78's shared
daily journal. The controller captures the consent account/context before asking;
the lazy voice driver reserves before opening a socket, rereads SecretStorage
following the durable writes, and checks account, context and budgets at each send.

Each recording accepts at most 180 seconds of 16 kHz mono 16-bit PCM and has an
elapsed deadline from stream creation, including admission waits. Windows,
macOS and Linux recorder chunks are normalized into 80 ms messages plus at most
one short tail. The reservation is 180,080 ms at $0.18/hour: $0.009004. The final
reported `audioProcessedMs` is validated as a nonnegative safe integer, floored
to whole seconds, and settled at the tariff. A 180,080 ms report settles $0.009;
a 2,000 ms report settles $0.0001. Reports are never silently clamped. Silence
counts. Provider behavior matching the captured framing is the assumption behind
the reserve bound; this is a tariff estimate, not a read-back of Meta's invoice.

Without a final report, the visible tally uses locally sent whole seconds and
sent audio retains the entire reservation as uncertain liability in both
journals. No audio sent refunds zero, including the captured idle error and
1008 close. Both settlements are attempted independently and a failed write is
shown to the user. Disposal during either durable write refunds once its claim
arrives. A throwing transport counts attempted dispatch conservatively and a
throwing close cannot prevent settlement. Late transcripts from an old account
or context never reach the composer.

Allow once is window-scoped for voice in the production registry. Workspace
Always, explicit false, feature availability, price acceptance and revocation
retain their existing gates. Each completed or interrupted recording emits a
localized duration/cost notice distinguishing reported duration, local estimate
and no sent audio. All mention the unavailable provider bill.
