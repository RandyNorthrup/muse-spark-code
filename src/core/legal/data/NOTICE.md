JSON whitespace was normalized with Prettier; identifier values were unchanged.
Exact source URLs, versions, acquisition date, original hashes, and vendored
hashes are recorded in `provenance.json`. Licenses named by the identifiers
retain their own terms. Runtime scans make no data downloads.

`spdxLicenseTexts.json` holds derived match data only (a normalized SHA-256,
a word count and short distinctive phrases for 15 licenses) computed from the
SPDX License List 3.28.0 texts shipped in `spdx-license-list` 6.12.0
(Sindre Sorhus, CC0-1.0 package; SPDX texts © their respective licensors).
No full license text is vendored. Every phrase was machine-checked present in
its canonical text at derivation time; the tarball hash, derivation method and
vendored hash are recorded in `provenance.json`.
